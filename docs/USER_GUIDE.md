# User Guide – Planora AI

Welcome to Planora AI! This guide walks you through planning your first event from initial concept to flawless execution.

---

## 1. Quick-Start Guide (5-Minute Workflow)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
- Navigate to `signup.html`.
- Enter your name, email, and a secure password (or click "Sign In with Google").
- Once signed in, you are automatically directed to your **Dashboard**.

### Step 2: Create Your Event Workspace
- Click **New Event** or navigate to `create-event.html`.
- Enter your event details:
  - **Name**: e.g., *"Aarav & Meera's Wedding Reception"*.
  - **Event Type**: Choose from Wedding, Birthday, Anniversary, Corporate, etc.
  - **Event Date**: Choose your target celebration date.
  - **Guest Count**: Enter your estimated attendee count.
  - **Total Budget & Currency**: Set your target spending cap (default is `INR (₹)`).
- Click **Create Event & Launch Studio**.

### Step 3: Generate Your Master Plan in AI Event Studio
- You are redirected to `planner.html` with your new event pre-selected.
- Specify your aesthetic preference (e.g. *"Royal Minimalist with floral arches"*).
- Choose your budget priority (e.g. *"Heavy on Venue & Gourmet Dining"*).
- Click **Generate Master Plan**.
- Review the generated plan:
  - Executive summary and color palette ideas.
  - Normalized budget allocation across 14 categories.
  - T-minus countdown milestones.
  - Hour-by-hour event-day schedule run-sheet.
  - Recommended vendor checklist and cost-saving tips.
- Click **Apply to Workspace** to automatically populate these items into your live workspace!

### Step 4: Track Expenses in Budget Management
- Navigate to `budget.html`.
- View your total budget, estimated allocations, and actual spent amount.
- As you pay vendor deposits, click **Add Expense** or click the pencil icon next to an existing item to enter actual paid amounts and check "Mark as Paid".
- Monitor your budget utilization bar and donut chart to ensure you stay in the green.

### Step 5: Execute with Task Management
- Navigate to `tasks.html`.
- Browse tasks generated by the AI Studio or add your own checklist items.
- Filter by status (**To Do**, **In Progress**, **Completed**) or priority.
- Check off tasks as you finish them; your dashboard completion percentage updates instantly.

### Step 6: Coordinate Vendors & RSVPs
- **Vendors (`vendors.html`)**: Log vendor quotes, contact numbers, and track booking statuses from *Quoted* to *Confirmed*.
- **Guests (`guests.html`)**: Record attendee names, RSVP confirmations, dietary requirements (e.g., Jain, Vegan), table zones, and +1 partner headcounts.

### Step 7: Export Executive Reports
- Navigate to `reports.html`.
- View your full event dossier and readiness metrics.
- Click **Print / PDF** for a printer-optimized run-sheet for your day-of coordinators.
- Download **CSV** files to share directly with your banquet caterers and decorators.
