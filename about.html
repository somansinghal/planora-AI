Our Mission

From Idea to Flawlessly Planned

We believe celebrating life's milestones shouldn't come with crippling stress, runaway budgets, or chaotic spreadsheets.

Why We Built Planora AI

Whether it is an Indian wedding spanning multiple days with hundreds of guests, a golden jubilee anniversary, or a corporate product launch, organizing events today requires juggling dozens of disparate apps: notes apps, spreadsheets, messy WhatsApp threads, and unverified vendor quotes.

Planora AI was created to provide a unified, intelligent event operating system. We combine bleeding-edge AI generation with deterministic, persistent SaaS workspaces: itemized financial tracking, T-minus milestone schedules, vendor contract trackers, and guest RSVP management.

We do not believe in fake AI fluff. Every budget allocation is mathematically validated, every suggestion is editable, and your private guest lists and financial figures remain strictly yours.

Our Architectural Principles

🛡️

Privacy & Authorization First

Every backend endpoint enforces cryptographic token verification and strict user-ownership checks. We never leak one host's guest list or budget to another.

🧠

Disciplined AI Architecture

AI is a co-pilot, not a black box. Generated plans use strict Pydantic schemas, server-side budget sum normalization, and explicit disclaimers regarding market vendor rates.

⚡

Ultra-Fast & Lightweight

Zero bloated JavaScript frameworks. Built on clean modern vanilla web standards, high-performance FastAPI asynchronous Python, and instant inference.

🇮🇳

Culturally Grounded

Built with deep appreciation for regional celebrations, multi-day wedding rituals (Mehendi, Sangeet, Reception), diverse dietary requirements (Jain, Halal, Vegan), and INR defaults.
