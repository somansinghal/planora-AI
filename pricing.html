Frequently Asked Questions

Is Planora AI really free to use?

Yes! You can plan an entire wedding or celebration on our Free tier without entering a credit card. All core features (AI Studio, budget breakdown, guest RSVP, timeline) are included.

What currencies do you support?

We prioritize the Indian market with INR (₹) by default, while fully supporting USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), AED (د.إ), and SGD (S$) with localized formatting.

How does the AI create budgets and timelines?

Planora AI uses high-performance large language models via Groq validated with strict Pydantic schemas. Percentages and estimated costs are verified server-side so allocations remain disciplined.
