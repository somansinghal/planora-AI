Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Crafted by Soman Singhal

1. Introduction & Project Status

Planora AI is an independent software application developed and maintained by full-stack engineer Soman Singhal. This Privacy Policy outlines our real-world data collection, processing, and storage practices.

Notice on Legal Review: This document is developer-drafted to accurately represent the application's actual data pipelines. It has not received formal independent legal counsel review. Users requiring custom enterprise data processing agreements (DPAs) should contact the developer.

2. Information We Collect & Sub-processors

We process only data required to deliver core event workspace functionality:

  • Account Identity: Email address, display name, and unique user ID (UID) managed via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Workspace & Operational Data: Event names, dates, budgets, itemized expenses, vendor details, and guest RSVP names stored in Google Cloud Firestore under strict per-user security rules.
  • AI Inference Inputs: Event descriptions and chat queries processed server-side via Groq Cloud (LLaMA 3.1 model). Prompts are used purely for generating immediate responses and are not sold or retained for third-party model training.

3. How We Protect Your Data

Your event workspaces are cryptographically isolated. Every database request enforces server-side token validation and resource ownership checks. We do not sell user data, run third-party advertising trackers, or share guest contact details.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain complete ownership of your data. When an event or user account is deleted, all associated expenses, tasks, guest lists, and AI plans are permanently purged from the database.

5. Contact & Privacy Requests

For privacy inquiries or data export requests, reach out directly to founder Soman Singhal via his verified Portfolio Contact or open an inquiry on GitHub.
