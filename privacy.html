Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Ownership

Planora AI ("we", "us", or "our") operates the event management platform accessible at planora.ai. We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and integrity of your personal information and event data.

Entity Information: [Legal Entity Name: Planora AI / To be supplied by Project Owner, Operating Address: To be supplied by Project Owner, Contact: privacy@planora.ai].

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our event planning SaaS:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and authentication tokens provided via Firebase Authentication.
  • Event Data: Event names, dates, budgets, guest counts, dietary notes, task items, vendor quotes, and itemized expenses.
  • AI Prompt Context: Text inputs provided to the AI Event Studio and Copilot to generate relevant event recommendations.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

Your event data is protected by server-side authorization controls and Firestore Security Rules. Every document is bound to your authenticated Firebase UID. We never sell, rent, or share your guest lists, contact details, or financial numbers with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on carefully audited infrastructure providers to operate the application:

  • Google Firebase: Managed authentication and Cloud Firestore database storage.
  • Groq Inc.: Inference provider for large language models used during AI plan generation. We do not permit AI providers to use your private event prompts for model retraining.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You retain 100% ownership of your data. When you delete an event or account within Planora AI, the associated expenses, tasks, vendors, and guest rosters are permanently purged from active collections.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding your personal data, please contact our team at privacy@planora.ai.
