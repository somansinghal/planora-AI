Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Crafted by Soman Singhal

1. Introduction & Ownership

Planora AI is an event management platform built and maintained by founder and software engineer Soman Singhal. We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and integrity of your personal information and celebration data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our event planning SaaS:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and authentication identifiers provided through secure Firebase Authentication.
  • Event Data: Event names, dates, budgets, guest counts, dietary notes, task items, vendor quotes, and itemized expenses.
  • AI Prompt Context: Text inputs provided to the AI Event Studio and Copilot to generate relevant event recommendations.

3. How We Use and Protect Data

Your event data is used solely to generate your plans, balance your budgets, coordinate tasks, and provide intelligent suggestions. We never sell your personal data or share private guest rosters with third-party advertisers.

All backend requests enforce token authentication and strict user-ownership validation to prevent unauthorized access across accounts.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain 100% ownership of your data. When you delete an event or account within Planora AI, the associated expenses, tasks, vendors, and guest rosters are permanently purged.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding your data, please contact the developer via Portfolio or GitHub.
